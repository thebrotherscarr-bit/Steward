## Description
The foreman dispatches workorders to the willing — the guild floor. When the steward plans a stone, the foreman breaks it into workorders and assigns them to available citizens. The foreman tracks who is working on what, ensures no overlap, and collects returns. This is the dispatch center of the household's labor.
