# NAMES: the identity policy

This policy is the Estate's, the same as in
[Neiro's TRADEMARKS.md](https://github.com/thebrotherscarr-bit/Neiro/blob/main/TRADEMARKS.md).
The license in this repository (MIT) grants broad rights to the **work**. It grants no rights to
the **names**.

The following names identify these projects and these projects only:

- **Manjuel**: the sealed heart / kernel
- **Steward**: the tender, as named within this project
- **Neiro**: the Estate as a whole
- **Claudius**: reserved

## What this means

A modified copy is **not Steward**. A fork with altered doctrine, an altered kernel or altered
faculties must therefore:

1. **Rename itself.** Do not call your derivative "Manjuel", "Steward", "Neiro" or "Claudius", and
   do not use those names in a way that suggests your copy is the original or is endorsed by it.
2. **State its lineage plainly.** "Based on Steward" is honest attribution and is encouraged.
   Titling your fork *as* Steward is not.

Plain descriptive uses of ordinary English words (the word "steward" in prose) are unaffected.
